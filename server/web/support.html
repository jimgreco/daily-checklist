Support

Ritual Cue

Practical help for restoring sync, managing your account, and sharing useful diagnostics without exposing checklist content.

Getting Help

If sync stops working, open Account and use Sync now. If changes are still pending, keep the app open on a stable connection and try again.

Account Requests

You can export your synced data, restore from a Ritual Cue JSON export, or delete your account from the Account screen. Restoring from an export replaces the signed-in account's synced checklist data. Deleting your account does not remove local offline copies from devices that are no longer connected.

Privacy Requests

Email privacy or data requests to support@ritualcue.com. Include the sign-in email for the account, but do not include sensitive checklist content in the message.

Contact

Email support requests to support@ritualcue.com and include the sign-in email for the account you need help with. You can also copy diagnostics from the Account screen; diagnostics include build, sync, device, and API-origin details, but not checklist content or tokens.
